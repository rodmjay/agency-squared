How it works

Four steps, all from a conversation.

Step 1

Connect

Add AgencyPro to your Claude once per agency. Run more than one? Each gets its own connection and sign-in, like separate email accounts.

Step 2

Ask

"Log three hours on the website project." "Draft this month's invoice." Your Claude reads and acts with your permissions.

Step 3

Hand off

Steps that need a licensed person go to a professional with a clear request, and their sign-off is recorded.

Step 4

Invoice

Send your customer an invoice with a payment link. They see the total, not the split.
